# market-news-alarm
AI agent that monitors SEC, Fed, CFTC, Federal Register, CNBC, Yahoo Finance and crypto news 24/7, uses Claude to rate each item 1-10 for potential market volatility, and pushes phone alerts above 7. Design driven by backtests on 2 years of news and price data (hourly and 1-minute, with fees), showing only rare, high-impact news is worth acting on.
